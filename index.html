03 / STEP ONE — CREATE ACCESS

Create your account.
Open your workspace.

Tap Get started, choose Create account, enter your name, email and password, then submit. Sign in after any verification the app requests.

WHERE TO TAPGet started → Create account → fill in your details → Create account.

04 / STEP TWO — SET UP THE BUSINESS

Give your business
its own home.

After signing in, create your company workspace. Enter the business name, choose the closest business type, and add optional details such as a motto or website. Then create the workspace.

WHAT TO ENTERCompany name → Business profile/type → optional details → Create workspace.

05 / STEP THREE — COMPLETE YOUR PROFILE

Make the profile
belong to your business.

From the workspace, open Business profile or the Business profile catalogue. Select the profile that best matches your work, open its available pages, and fill in the business details requested there. Save each form before moving on.

WHERE TO TAPHamburger menu → Business profile / Business profile catalogue → choose profile → add details → Save.

06 / STEP FOUR — SET UP YOUR TEAM

Bring in your people.
Set clear access.

Open Staff Management to add or invite team members using the options available in your workspace. Enter the requested staff details, choose an appropriate role or permissions, and save or send the invitation. Give each person only the access they need.

WHERE TO TAPHamburger menu → Staff Management → Add / Invite staff → enter details → assign role → Save / Send.

07 / STEP FIVE — ADD YOUR DAILY RECORDS

Turn daily activity
into organised records.

Open the area you need—Customers, Inventory, Products, Sales, Finance, Tasks or Operations. Tap its add/create action, complete the fields shown, check the information, and save. Start with a few accurate records and build from there.

WHERE TO TAPHamburger menu → choose a work area → Add / New record → complete fields → Save.

08 / STEP SIX — REVIEW AND KEEP MOVING

Check progress.
Choose your next step.

Use Overview for a quick look, Reports to review available summaries, Tasks to follow work, and Announcements to keep your team informed. Revisit your profile as your business changes, and confirm important changes were saved.

WHERE TO TAPOverview → Reports / Tasks / Announcements → review results → update records when needed.

05 · SECURITY, WITHOUT FALSE PROMISES

Your business data deserves careful protection.

Molan Business uses an authentication service and is designed for permission-controlled access. Security also depends on correctly configured database policies, safe account practices and regular testing.

  • Use a strong, unique password and protect your sign-in details.
  • Give staff only the access they need.
  • Sign out on shared devices and report anything suspicious.
  • No application can honestly guarantee zero risk; safeguards must be configured and reviewed.
SECURITY IS A SHARED RESPONSIBILITY Safeguards matter. So do safe habits.
06 · PLANS, UPDATES & UPGRADES

Know what you are paying for before you upgrade.

When billing is enabled, the app should show the plan, billing period, country-specific amount, currency, payment method and renewal/expiry details before you confirm.

  • Review plan features and the exact amount first.
  • Use a published payment option and keep the receipt/reference.
  • Online payments should activate a plan only after verified confirmation; authorised staff can handle approved manual upgrades.
  • Use the app's update action to check for new releases when available.
CLEAR PLANS. CLEAR CONFIRMATION. Never pay based on an unverified promise.
07 · WORKSHOP REAL TIME · OWNER-CONTROLLED

Keep an eye on your workshop, even when you are away.

Connect a compatible security camera or recorder to follow activity at your business in real time where the manufacturer supports secure live viewing. Available recordings can be reviewed with their recorded date and time.

  • The business owner authorises the camera and manages connections.
  • Use a supported IP camera/NVR/DVR, compatible ONVIF service, or approved manufacturer cloud/API.
  • Verify device ownership through the supported provider pairing process. A QR code, serial number or Bluetooth discovery alone does not prove ownership.
  • Keep the camera powered and connected to reliable internet; configure recorder/storage, timestamps and retention.
  • Grant staff camera-view permission only where needed. Only the business owner can delete or change archived footage.

Security controls are designed to protect access, but real protection depends on correct device setup, account security, permissions and the supported camera connection.

WORKSHOP REAL TIME Owner-controlled access. Timestamped footage.
