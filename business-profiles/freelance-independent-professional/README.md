# Freelance / independent professional profile

This profile focuses on client projects, deliverables, professional service delivery, quotes/invoices, portfolio media and reporting. Shared app modules are accessed through the authenticated Molan Business runtime and remain scoped to the active company workspace.

## Feature routes
- Client projects & deliverables → operations
- Client directory → customers
- Quotes, invoices & payments → finance
- Tasks & deadlines → tasks
- Appointments & service delivery → business-tools
- Portfolio & website media → website-studio
- Reports & insights → reports
- Team & collaborators → team
- Announcements → announcements

Shared authentication, authorization and data access stay in the application shell. Domain-specific CRUD remains subject to the active workspace permissions and Supabase RLS.
